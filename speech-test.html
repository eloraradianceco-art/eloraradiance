Read-Aloud Check

Find out which way of reading aloud works on this phone or browser. It takes about a minute.

  1. Turn the volume up.
  2. Turn on Silent Mode. iPhone: use the switch on the side or the Action button. iPad: Control Center → the bell.
  3. Tap each Play button below, one at a time. After each one, tap Heard it or No sound.
  4. Turn Silent Mode off and go through the buttons again.
  5. Tap Copy results and paste them into the chat.
