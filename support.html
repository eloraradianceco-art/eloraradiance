Help & Support

For Anchored Steps, Armed & Anchored, The Red Letters, Anchored Verse and The Living Planner

Email us at support@eloraradiance.com. Tell us which app you're using and the email on your account (or the email you paid with), and we'll take it from there.

1. Unlocking a purchase

Anchored Steps Year 1, Anchored Steps Year 2 and Armed & Anchored are one-time purchases with lifetime access. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

  • After you pay, you're returned to the app and it unlocks on its own.
  • Sign in with the same email you used at checkout. That's how the app matches your purchase to your account.
  • If an app still shows as locked a minute after paying, refresh the page. In Anchored Steps, tap I've Paid · Check Again.
  • Still locked? Email us with the email you paid with, and we'll unlock it for you.

2. Your account

  • One account for four apps: Anchored Steps Year 1, Year 2, Armed & Anchored and The Red Letters share one Elora Radiance account, so the same email and password work in all four. Each paid app still needs its own purchase.
  • The Living Planner has its own account. Anchored Verse needs no account.
  • Forgot your password? Choose Forgot Password on the sign-in screen, and we'll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
  • Using it like an app: open the app in Safari (iPhone/iPad) or Chrome (Android), then choose Add to Home Screen.

3. Billing questions

  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Charges appear on your statement as ELORA RADIANCE CO.
  • For a charge you don't recognize, a receipt, or to cancel or change a Living Planner Pro subscription, email us. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank, so we can make it right quickly.
  • Refunds and cancellations are covered in our Terms of Service.

4. Daily reminders

  • Turn them on in Settings → Daily Reminder, choose a time, and use Send a test to check them.
  • On iPhone and iPad, reminders work only after you add the app to your Home Screen and open it from there.

5. Deleting your account

In Anchored Steps Year 1, Year 2, Armed & Anchored or The Red Letters, go to Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete Account. This deletes your one shared account and everything in it. For The Living Planner, or if you'd rather we do it, email us. See our Privacy Policy for details.
