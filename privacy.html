Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Elora Radiance Co. LLC (“Elora Radiance,” “we”) makes Scripture-based apps: Anchored Steps Year 1, Anchored Steps Year 2, Armed & Anchored, The Red Letters, Anchored Verse and The Living Planner, plus this website. In short:

  • We collect only what the apps need to work.
  • We never sell your information, show ads, or share it for advertising.
  • What you write in your journals is private to your account.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, right in the app.

1. What we collect

Anchored Steps Year 1, Year 2, Armed & Anchored and The Red Letters share one Elora Radiance account, so one email and password works in all four. The Living Planner has its own account. Anchored Verse has no account at all.

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash that no one, including us, can read.
  • When you signed up and last signed in.

What you write and do in the apps

  • Journal entries, reflections, prayers, notes and answers you type.
  • Your progress: daily steps, weeks, weapons, passages, memorized verses and streaks.
  • Saved verses and settings such as light or dark mode.
  • In The Living Planner: the plans, tasks, events, goals, habits, budget categories and wellness entries you choose to enter.
  • In Anchored Verse: verses you save stay in your browser on your own device. They are never sent to us.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your email, what you bought, the amount and a Stripe reference number so we can unlock your app. We never see or store your card number. Your purchase is linked to the account that uses the email you paid with.

Daily reminders (only if you turn them on)

  • On the website or the app saved to your Home Screen: the reminder time you choose, your time zone, and a delivery address for that device from your browser's notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). That address lets us send the reminder; it contains no personal details.
  • In the iPhone or Android app: reminders are scheduled on your device. Your reminder time stays on the device.

Reviews and the newsletter

  • If you write a review: your rating, title, text, the name you choose to show, and your email. Your email is never published.
  • If you join our newsletter: your email, which page you joined from, and your browser type.

Website analytics and technical information

  • eloraradiance.com uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It uses no cookies and does not identify you.
  • Like any website, our hosting providers process your IP address and basic device information to deliver pages and keep services secure.

2. How we use it

  • To run the apps: sign you in, save your work and keep it in sync across your devices, and unlock what you bought.
  • To send messages you asked for: password reset emails, daily reminders, and the newsletter if you joined.
  • To support and improve the apps: answer your questions, fix problems, and understand overall use, such as how many people signed up or signed in.
  • To keep things safe and legal: prevent abuse and keep the purchase records tax law requires.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use what you write to train AI. There are no ads in our apps.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account and app data: for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, it is deleted right away from our live database and from backups within 7 days.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account, but no longer linked to it.
  • Reviews: deleted along with your account.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in Anchored Steps Year 1, Year 2, Armed & Anchored or The Red Letters, go to Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete Account. This deletes your one shared account and everything in it in all four apps. For The Living Planner, or if you'd rather we do it, email us and we'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data: use Export in Settings to download your journal, or email us for a full copy.
  • Correct your information: email us.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Settings or in your device's notification settings at any time.
  • Newsletter: email us to unsubscribe.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the European Union or the United Kingdom) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to how it's used. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live, and respond within 30 days.

6. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and our providers encrypt it at rest. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

8. Where your data is stored

Our services are run from the United States. If you use the apps from another country, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also let you know in the apps or by email.
