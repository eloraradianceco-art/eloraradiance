What Makes It Different

More Than a Planner. A System.

Not another minimal to-do app. Not a bloated Notion template you have to assemble yourself. The Living Planner is a complete, opinionated system designed around the rhythms of an actual stewardship-driven life.

🌅
DAY VIEW + LIFE SCORE + WORD OF THE YEAR
Open the app and see today's priorities, today's habits, your Life Score across all eight domains, and the word you're orienting your year around — all on the home screen. No fishing for what matters.
🎯
GOAL HORIZONS
Track goals across seven horizons — 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, and all. SMART framework guidance built in. Goals that don't fade past January.
📅
CALENDAR + TASKS + PROJECTS
Daily and weekly calendar views, drag-and-drop tasks, and project tracking — all interlinked. The infrastructure that frees you from sticky notes and reminders apps.
🙏
FAITH AS INFRASTRUCTURE
Eight built-in faith tools — devotional, prayer list, scripture, reading plan, gratitude journal, fasting tracker, sermon notes, spiritual goals. Your relationship with God doesn't live in a separate app.
💸
STEWARDSHIP FINANCE
Eight finance tabs covering overview, income, expenses, budget, savings goals, debt, donations, and no-spend challenges. Sinking funds, net-worth history, generous-first defaults — built for stewards.
🩺
WHOLE-PERSON WELLNESS
Mood, sleep, anxiety, journal, meds, migraines, coping skills, metrics. Workouts tracked in Lifestyle. Track the body alongside the soul — they aren't separate.
☁️
REAL-TIME SYNC
Update on your phone, see it on your iPad in real time. Supabase realtime under the hood — every device stays current without a refresh.
🔔
CONFIGURABLE PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Habit reminders, prayer prompts, daily check-ins. Configurable per habit so you control the signal, not the noise.
