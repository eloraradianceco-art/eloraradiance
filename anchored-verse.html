How It Works

Simple. Fast. Anchored.

Designed for the moments you actually need it — not after twenty taps and a sign-up flow. Open it. Pick a feeling. Read the verse. Carry it with you.

🫀
Pick the Feeling
Start at the center of the Feelings Wheel with the core emotion, then tap the word for what you're actually carrying. No journaling, no mood scale. Prefer a list? Switch with one tap.
📖
Get the Anchor
A Scripture matched to that emotion appears immediately — with a short reflection that doesn't just speak to the feeling, but reframes it.
🔄
Three Translations
NKJV, ESV, or NIV. Toggle at the top — your choice persists across every emotion, so the Word reads in the voice that resonates with you.
⚓
Save Your Anchors
Tap Save anchor on any verse to keep it. Build your own collection of the Scriptures that have anchored you, kept on your device whenever you need to return to them.
↗
Share Verse Cards
Tap share to generate a beautifully styled card with the verse, reference, and emotion category. Light or dark theme. Send to a friend, post to Instagram, or save the image.
⚡
No Account Needed
Open the app and start. No sign-up, no credit card, no friction between you and the next verse you need.
